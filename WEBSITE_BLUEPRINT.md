# JIHOPE TECH - Website Blueprint

## Overview
JIHOPE TECH is positioned as a premium technology transformation platform. The website must communicate trust, capability, reliability, and strategic value. It is not just a portfolio site; it is a conversion engine for business trust and client acquisition.

---

## Website Objectives
- Position JIHOPE TECH as a premium technology partner
- Show credibility, technical capability, and business understanding
- Convert visitors into leads and consultation calls
- Highlight service value, process, and delivery quality
- Build a strong portfolio focused on business outcomes

---

## Target Audience
- Business owners
- SMEs and growing enterprises
- NGOs and institutions
- Startups needing digital infrastructure
- Organizations needing transformation, automation, or software upgrades

---

## Recommended Brand Message for Homepage
JIHOPE TECH helps businesses improve operations, strengthen digital presence, and build secure, reliable systems that support long-term growth.

---

## Design Direction
- Hero background: dark navy with blue accents
- Buttons: Electric Blue with strong contrast
- Cards: minimal white boxes with subtle shadows
- Typography: bold headings, modern sans-serif body
- Icons: streamlined geometric system icons
- Layout: clean professional grid, lots of whitespace

---

## Website Copy Style
Use direct, confident, professional language.

Good examples:
- Reliable technology solutions for growing businesses
- Secure systems, strategic design, and measurable outcomes
- Transforming businesses through digital innovation

Avoid:
- vague, trendy buzzwords
- low-value generic service language
- overpromising without proof

---

## Recommended Tech Stack
For development of the portfolio website:
- Frontend: React or Next.js
- Styling: Tailwind CSS
- CMS or content: Sanity or MDX
- Deployment: Vercel
- Analytics: Google Analytics / Plausible
- Form handling: Formspree or custom email service
