# JIHOPE TECH - Service Packages & Pricing Framework

## Positioning
JIHOPE TECH should not be priced like a cheap generic web design service. Our model should reflect strategy, technology capability, reliability, and long-term business value.

Pricing should feel premium, but flexible enough to attract businesses at different stages.

---

## Recommended Pricing Strategy
Use a value-based approach rather than a simple hourly rate.

Factors influencing price:
- scope complexity
- business size
- integrations and data requirements
- required security measures
- expected timeline
- long-term maintenance and support
- strategic consulting involvement

## Pricing Philosophy
You should position pricing around:
- business value
- reliability and trust
- technical quality
- future scalability
- security and long-term support

Avoid pricing only on appearance or low-cost template delivery. JIHOPE TECH is not a cheap website factory; it is a technology transformation partner.

---

## Recommended Revenue Model
A balanced model can include:
- Project-based builds
- Retainer-based maintenance and support
- Consulting engagements
- Custom software contracts
- Automation and optimization projects
- Long-term digital partner subscriptions

This creates stronger cash flow and better client retention.
